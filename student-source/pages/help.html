Help & Guidelines

Learn how to report, search, and claim items on HeronSearch.

Quick Guides

How to report a lost item

  1. Log in to your account.
  2. Click Report Item and choose I Lost an Item.
  3. Fill in the item name, category, location, date, and description.
  4. Submit. An admin will review your report before it is posted.

How to report a found item

  1. Log in and click Report Item.
  2. Choose I Found an Item.
  3. Describe where and when you found it.
  4. Keep the item safe until an admin or the owner contacts you.

How to claim an item

  1. Open Found Items and find your item.
  2. Click Claim Item.
  3. Describe the item and upload proof of ownership.
  4. Wait for the admin to approve your claim. Check My Claims for updates.

Guidelines

  • Post only real items and give honest, accurate details.
  • Do not post personal information in the description (such as ID numbers).
  • Only claim items that really belong to you.
  • Be respectful when contacting other users.
  • Reports that are false or abusive may be removed by the admin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use HeronSearch?

Yes. You need an account to report items, search, and submit claims. Create one on the welcome page.

Why is my report not showing yet?

Every report is reviewed by an admin first. It will appear in the listings once it is approved.

How do I know if my claim was approved?

You will get a notification, and you can also check the status in My Claims.

What proof of ownership can I upload?

A photo of the item, a receipt, or anything that shows the item is yours.

Where do I get my item after the claim is approved?

The admin will let you know where and when you can pick it up on campus.

Still need help?

Contact the HeronSearch team and we will get back to you.
