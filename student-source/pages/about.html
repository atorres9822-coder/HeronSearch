About HeronSearch

The UMak community platform that helps students and staff reconnect with their lost belongings.

Our Mission

HeronSearch makes it easier to report, find, and return lost items on campus. Instead of asking around or posting in group chats, everything is in one place, so lost items get back to their rightful owners faster.

How It Works

1

Report

Post a lost or found item with its details, location, and date.

2

Search

Browse the listings or search by category, location, and date.

3

Claim

Submit a claim with proof of ownership and wait for verification.

Why HeronSearch

One Central Place

All lost and found reports in the university, easy to browse.

Verified Claims

Admins review claims so items go back to the real owners.

Made for UMak

Built by UMak students for the UMak community.
