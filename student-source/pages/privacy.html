Privacy Policy

This page explains what information HeronSearch collects, why we collect it, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your full name and email address.
  • Report details: item name, category, location, date, description, photos, and the contact information you provide.
  • Claim details: your description of the item and the proof of ownership you upload.

How we use it

  • To post lost and found reports and help items return to their owners.
  • To let admins verify claims before an item is released.
  • To send you notifications about your reports and claims.

Who can see it

Item details and the contact information in a report are visible to other HeronSearch users once the report is approved. Proof of ownership is visible only to admins reviewing your claim.

Your responsibilities

Do not post personal information, such as ID numbers, in item descriptions or photos.

Your rights

You may ask to view, correct, or delete your information. We handle personal data in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Questions

Send us a message through the Contact page.
